# Changelog

## 1.1.0

First public release, for Midnight 12.1 (Interface 120100).

- Eight target markers, clear, ready check and pull countdown in one bar.
- Plain click places the target marker, modifier click the world marker.
- Options panel under Blizzard's Settings.
- Visibility by state driver: always, in a group, or in a raid.
- Dims in a raid without lead or assist.
